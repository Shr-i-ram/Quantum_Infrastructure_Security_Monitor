# Quantum Infrastructure Security Monitor (QISM)
## Publication Visualization Catalogue

This catalogue documents all publication-grade figures, architectural schematics, training curves, and performance trade-off visualizations generated by the QISM benchmark suite in `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/`.

All figures are generated in both **High-Resolution PNG (300 DPI)** and **Vector PDF** formats for research papers, presentations, and archival documentation.

## 2. Detailed Figure Specifications

### FIG-01: Overall In-Distribution AUROC Comparison
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/01_overall_auroc_comparison.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/01_overall_auroc_comparison.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/aggregate_metrics.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Ranks all evaluated models. Highlights the top performance of **`ClassicalTemporalAutoencoder` ($0.7616$)**, **`QuantumKernelSVM_4Q` ($0.7570$)**, **`ClassicalLogisticRegression` ($0.7408$)**, and **`PITQSVDD_4Q_Amplitude` ($0.7147$)**.
- **Color Coding**: Blue for Classical Baselines, Purple for Quantum Baselines, Orange/Red for Flagship PI-TQSVDD.

### FIG-02: Operational Recall at 1% False Positive Rate
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/02_recall_at_1pct_fpr_comparison.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/02_recall_at_1pct_fpr_comparison.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/aggregate_metrics.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Evaluates true attack detection recall when the decision threshold is calibrated strictly on normal validation telemetry to allow at most 1 false alarm per 100 benign operating windows. `ClassicalLogisticRegression` achieves **$40.3\%$**, `Mahalanobis_Full` achieves **$40.0\%$**, `ClassicalAutoencoder` achieves **$39.8\%$**, and `PITQSVDD_4Q_Amplitude` achieves **$38.2\%$**.

### FIG-03: Performance vs. Compute Cost Pareto Frontier
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/03_performance_vs_compute_frontier.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/comparisons/03_performance_vs_compute_frontier.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: Merged `aggregate_metrics.csv` and `compute_cost_table.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Visualizes the trade-off between AUROC and per-window inference latency on a logarithmic time scale. Shows that classical models occupy the ultra-fast sub-millisecond regime ($0.02 - 0.42$ ms), while quantum models require $1.0 - 8.6$ ms in simulation.

### FIG-04: PI-TQSVDD Qubit Count Sweep
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/04_qubit_count_sweep.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/04_qubit_count_sweep.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/pitqsvdd_ablation_table.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Demonstrates that 4 qubits is the optimal operating point. Left panel shows AUROC peaking at 4 qubits ($0.6751$) and decreasing at 8 qubits ($0.5908$). Right panel shows training wall-clock time increasing steadily from 2 to 8 qubits on `lightning.qubit`.

### FIG-05: Quantum Data Encoding Comparison
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/05_encoding_comparison.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/05_encoding_comparison.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/pitqsvdd_ablation_table.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Compares five quantum encoding families on 4 qubits. Amplitude Encoding achieves the highest AUROC ($0.7147$), followed by Data Reuploading ($0.6795$) and Angle Embedding ($0.6751$), whereas IQP/ZZ phase encoding fails ($0.4838$).

### FIG-06: Architecture and Temporal Loss Ablation
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/06_temporal_ablation_comparison.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/pitqsvdd/06_temporal_ablation_comparison.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/pitqsvdd_ablation_table.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Direct ablation comparing Full PI-TQSVDD ($\lambda_t = 0.5$, AUROC $0.6751$) against Static SVDD ($\lambda_t = 0.0$, AUROC $0.6453$) and Baseline Static Quantum SVDD ($0.6402$), proving that temporal transition dynamics provide a quantifiable performance gain.

### FIG-07: Generalization Robustness Across Regimes
- **Paths**:
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/generalization/07_generalization_robustness.png`
  - `results/qism_model_benchmark/figures/generalization/07_generalization_robustness.pdf`
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI, High-Resolution PNG & Vector PDF.
- **Data Source**: `results/qism_model_benchmark/tables/aggregate_metrics.csv`
- **Key Insight**: Compares detection performance across in-distribution, held-out generalization, and stress partitions for key models. Shows that normal-only models (Classical Autoencoder, Mahalanobis, and PI-TQSVDD) generalize robustly to unseen transmission distances ($>0.77$ AUROC).
